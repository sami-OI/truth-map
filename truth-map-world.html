Ontology reasoning
Pick a Story or change an axis. The reasoning here updates to match the entity relationship you are looking at.
Activation crisis: "Never logged in" means the Learner entity exists but no session was ever opened. The chain broke before the learner could reach any course, so the barrier is device access or onboarding, not the curriculum.
Gender as County property: female % is a relational property of (County × Programme), not of female learners. The County's social context is the explanatory variable, not learner capability. Compare registered vs enrolled female % to see where women drop out of the chain.
Reach vs enrolment: registeredUsers counts Learners in a County, while enrolledUsers counts Enrollment entities for the selected Course. A big gap means learners are on the platform but the Learner→Course relationship hasn't been created yet.
Dropout risk: the score is computed in the browser from five structural signals: never logged in (35), logged in but not enrolled (25), enrolled but not completing (20, estimated from the course's age-group completion), female access gap (10) and low female share (10). The biggest signal names the action.
Age groups: age_group is a Learner-dimension property. When you cross it with Enrollment, you can see whether conversion depends on the learner's life stage. Check the Courses tab to see completion split by age group.

Dropout risk — computed in the browser ·
Score 0–100 per county and per county × age group, from five structural signals: never logged in (35 pts) · logged in but not enrolled (25) · enrolled but not completing (20, estimated from this course's completion rate by age group) · female access gap vs male (10) · female share below 45% (10). Bands: high ≥ 45 · medium ≥ 25 · low < 25. The largest signal decides the action code.
Risk band distribution

Access vs completion

County name cleanup — values as entered → clean county
The county field is free text. Before any chart is drawn, values are matched to the official county list: exact name, then known sub-county or constituency, then "County + suffix" (e.g. MANDERA EAST), then a one- or two-letter typo match. Values that aren't a place (organisations, roles, phone numbers) go to Unspecified, and those are the ones to fix at source. Blue values come from learners whose country was “Unknown” (or another unmapped value) but whose county is in this country.
